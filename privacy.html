What we collect

Consultation request form (in the chat assistant)

Your name, email address, business type, the service you are interested in, your project details and, if you choose, a preferred consultation date and time of day. We also record when you gave consent and which version of this notice you agreed to.

Emails about your request

When you submit the form, your request details are emailed to our own Gmail inbox. We may also email a short receipt to the address you gave, containing only your reference number and first name. A receipt may not be sent, for example if one was already sent to that address in the last 24 hours or if our daily sending limit was reached. The receipt only confirms we received your request; it is not a confirmed appointment.

Chat assistant

Your chat messages are processed to generate replies. We do not save chat conversations. Please do not share sensitive personal information in the chat.

Security

A scrambled, one-way code made from your IP address, used only to limit abuse of the chat and form. It cannot be turned back into your IP address. Each code is used for no more than 24 hours to apply these limits. Codes that are no longer in use are not yet deleted automatically; we delete them manually. We use Cloudflare Turnstile to block automated spam.

Why we collect it

  • To answer your inquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To contact you about a consultation, if you request one.
  • To protect the website from spam and abuse.

We do not sell your information and do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Legal basis

We use this information because you agree to it when you submit the form, and to protect the website from spam and abuse.

Consultation requests are not confirmed appointments

A preferred date and time you send is a request only. It is not a booking and does not mean that time is available. We will contact you to confirm a date and time or to suggest another one.

Who processes it for us

  • Cloudflare, Inc.: website hosting, database, AI processing of chat messages, and spam protection.
  • Google LLC: Gmail and Google Apps Script, used to email your request to us and to send you a receipt.

These providers may store or process data in other countries. Their own privacy policies explain how they handle data: Cloudflare, Google.

How long we keep it

  • Inquiries and consultation requests: requests older than 12 months are removed from our database whenever a new request is submitted. You can ask us to delete yours sooner at any time.
  • Emails about your request in our Gmail account: we review these every month and delete those that have reached 12 months. Because deletion is done by hand, an email may be kept a little longer than 12 months before it is deleted.
  • Security codes made from IP addresses: used for no more than 24 hours, then deleted manually.
  • Chat messages: not stored.

If you become a client, project records are handled separately under our client agreement.
