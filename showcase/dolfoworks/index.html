What we do

Our Services

Professional automotive care, detailing, upgrades, and body works for cars and motorcycles.

DIY Carwash

  • Self-service wash bay for cars and motorcycles
  • Wash your own ride inside the DolfoWorks bay

Motorcycle & Auto Detailing

  • Exterior detailing: premium wash, clay bar treatment, buffing, hydrophobic wax
  • Removal of iron/fallout, asphalt, acid rain, watermarks, swirl marks and light scratches
  • Graphene ceramic coating
  • Mags restoration
  • Panel buffing

Accessories Upgrade

  • Senlo X3 and Senlo X1 installation
  • Bosch Europa, including rewiring
  • DRL, LED fog light, H4 and LED headlight upgrades
  • Foot, ambient, side pillar and bumper lights
  • Honeycomb grill
  • Steel skid plate installation

Maintenance

  • Preventive maintenance service (PMS)
  • Engine oil and oil filter change

Tint

  • Window tint installation for cars

Carbon Fiber

  • Steering wheel carbon panel

Paint & Body Works

  • Panel repaints for cars
  • Motorcycle outer fairing repaints
